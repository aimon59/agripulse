    Broiler chicken · Batch B

    Suspected: Coccidiosis (early stage)
    Medium

    Likely cause

    Damp, overcrowded litter combined with recent temperature swings has likely triggered an outbreak of intestinal parasites common at 2–4 weeks of age.

    Recommended action

    Isolate visibly affected birds today. Start an anticoccidial (e.g. amprolium) in drinking water for 5–7 days at the dose on the label, and replace wet litter in the affected pen.

    Feed / medicine plan

    Add a vitamin K + electrolyte supplement for 3 days to offset fluid loss. Re-check droppings on day 3; if blood persists, involve your local vet.
